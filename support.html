Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is Special Sport Analysis?

Special Sport Analysis is a sports analytics and statistical modeling mobile app that delivers daily algorithmically generated match previews, historical head-to-head performance ratings, strategy insights, and statistical trends for Football (Soccer), Tennis, and Basketball.

2. How do In-App Purchases and digital unlocks work?

Users can unlock select premium match analyses (such as Single Pick and Special Combo analyses). Unlocks are processed directly and securely by Apple In-App Purchase (or Google Play). Once unlocked, you gain full instant access to the detailed statistical breakdown for that match.

3. How do I restore or sync my purchases across multiple devices?

To access your unlocked analyses on multiple iOS devices, simply sign in with your registered account in the app's Settings screen. All active unlocks will automatically sync from our secure cloud infrastructure.

4. How do I request a refund for an In-App Purchase?

Since in-app payments are processed directly by Apple, refund requests are governed by Apple's standard policy. You can request a refund by visiting reportaproblem.apple.com with your Apple ID.

5. Is Special Sport Analysis a gambling or betting app?

No. Special Sport Analysis is strictly an informational and statistical tool. We do not accept bets, facilitate wagers, operate bookmaking services, or provide betting platforms.

6. How do I request account deletion or data removal?

You can request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data anytime by sending an email to support@specialsportanalysis.com or submitting a request via the in-app Contact Us form with subject "Account Deletion".
