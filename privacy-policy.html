Privacy Policy

How Special Sport Analysis Collects, Uses, and Safeguards Your Information

Last Updated: September 30, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Introduction & Overview

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how Special Sport Analysis ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you download, install, and use our mobile application across iOS and Android platforms.

2. Information We Collect

We prioritize minimal data collection, gathering only what is strictly required to provide sports analysis and preserve your purchases:

  • Voluntary Account Data: If you choose to register an account (optional), we collect your email address and preferred display name to authenticate you and sync your unlocked tips across authorized devices.
  • Anonymous Device Identifiers: Non-personally identifiable tokens used for local unlock persistence and in-app purchase receipts processed through RevenueCat.
  • User Preferences: Selected language, notification settings, and saved sports category preferences stored locally on your device.
  • Diagnostics & Analytics: Anonymous crash reports, device model, and operating system version to maintain high performance and fix bugs.
  • No Third-Party Ad Trackers: We do not use third-party behavioral advertising networks or video ad tracking SDKs.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for legitimate application operations, including:

  • Delivering match previews, statistical projections, and expert sports insights;
  • Validating and synchronizing your digital unlocks (Single Pick Analysis and Special Combo Analysis);
  • Dispatching opt-in push notifications for new match insights and tournament updates;
  • Processing your inquiries and feedback submitted via our in-app Contact Us form;
  • Maintaining platform reliability, preventing abuse, and ensuring app security.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We collaborate with industry-leading, privacy-compliant service providers to operate our app infrastructure:

  • Supabase: Secure cloud database and encrypted authentication.
  • RevenueCat & Apple App Store / Google Play: Secure processing of digital in-app purchases and receipt validation.
  • OneSignal / Firebase Cloud Messaging: Reliable delivery of push notification alerts (only when enabled by you).

5. Data Security & Encryption

We employ robust industry-standard security measures, including SSL/TLS encrypted data transmission and AES encryption for data at rest. Passwords are never stored in plaintext and sensitive transaction processing is handled directly by Apple and Google.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

In compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and applicable global privacy laws, you have full control over your personal data:

  • You can inspect, update, or correct your profile data at any time;
  • You may request complete deletion of your account and cloud records by submitting a request via our in-app Contact Us screen or emailing us directly.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Application is designed for users aged 18 and older (or legal age of majority). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact our support team immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or legal requirements. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
